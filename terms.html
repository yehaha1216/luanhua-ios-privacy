1. 服务内容

本应用提供本地记账、统计、预算、余额、主题、数据导入导出、语音输入、图片识别和可选 AI 辅助记账。iOS 支持可选 iCloud 同步与“嘟嘎截屏记账”快捷指令；iOS / OpenHarmony 在登录 Apple / 华为账号并连接后端服务后使用 AI 和额度服务；Android 无需账号，也不提供后端服务额度。

部分系统能力、第三方服务或模型可能因地区、设备、版本或服务商状态而不可用。

1. What the service covers

The App provides local bookkeeping, statistics, budgets, balances, themes, data import/export, voice input, image recognition, and optional AI-assisted bookkeeping. iOS supports optional iCloud Sync and the “Duga Screenshot Ledger” shortcut. On iOS / OpenHarmony, AI and credits are available after signing in with Apple / Huawei and connecting to the backend service. Android requires no account and offers no backend service credits.

Some system capabilities, third-party services, or models may be unavailable by region, device, version, or provider status.

账本和设置默认保存在你的设备上。你应自行核对、维护并备份重要数据；删除会话、清除应用数据、卸载应用或设备故障可能导致本地数据丢失。

导出的备份是明文 JSON，可包含账本、分类、预算、余额快照、主题、AI 规则、自动分类和 AI 显示设置，并带有格式版本、导出平台、应用版本和导出时间等元数据；Android 备份还可包含自定义模型配置，其中 API Key 为明文，取得文件的人可能读取该密钥。导入备份可能覆盖现有数据，请确认来源并妥善保管或删除备份文件。

2. Local data and backups

Ledger data and settings are stored on your device by default. You are responsible for checking, maintaining, and backing up important data; deleting sessions, clearing app data, uninstalling the App, or device failure may cause local data loss.

Exported backups are plaintext JSON and may contain ledger data, categories, budgets, balance snapshots, themes, AI rules, auto-category settings, and AI display settings, with metadata such as the format version, export platform, app version, and export time. Android backups may also contain custom model configuration, whose API Key is plaintext, so anyone who obtains the file may read that key. Importing a backup may overwrite current data; verify its source and securely store or delete the file.

Before exporting a backup with model configuration, the App warns about the API Key risk. The file is written only after you confirm “Export anyway”; cancel aborts the export.

AI 辅助记账是可选功能。Android 使用你配置的兼容模型提供商；iOS / OpenHarmony 使用后端服务下发的 AI 网关配置。

AI 工具可以直接创建、修改或删除账本记录，不会逐条等待你确认；模型输出可能不准确、不完整或不符合预期，仅供参考。你应在使用后核对账本、分类、预算和余额，并对基于输出作出的决定负责。

3. AI-assisted bookkeeping

AI-assisted bookkeeping is optional. Android uses the compatible model provider you configure; iOS / OpenHarmony use the AI gateway configuration issued by the backend service.

AI tools can directly create, update, or delete ledger records without a per-record confirmation step. Model output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unexpected and is for reference only. Check ledger entries, categories, budgets, and balances after use, and you are responsible for decisions based on the output.

Do not enter unlawful, infringing, or unnecessary sensitive information. Model fees, availability, and data processing are also subject to the backend service or the relevant model provider's rules.

4. 平台账号、注销与数据

Android 无需账号。iOS / OpenHarmony 的账号由 Apple / 华为登录和后端服务账户共同构成。你应妥善保护设备和账号，不得出借、转让或冒用他人账号。

登录时，应用会向后端服务发送身份凭据、设备标识（iOS IDFV / OpenHarmony ODID）和应用版本。退出登录会清除本地账户登录会话并锁定 AI。

iOS / OpenHarmony 用户可在账户页注销后端服务账号。注销会清除账户会话和待核验票据，未使用的额度无法恢复；本地账本、iCloud 数据及 Android 自定义模型配置不会因注销而删除。iCloud 数据和远端平台账户数据需按 Apple、华为或后端服务的规则单独管理。

4. Platform accounts, deletion, and data

Android requires no account. On iOS / OpenHarmony, the account combines Apple / Huawei sign-in and a backend service account. Safeguard your device and account, and do not lend, transfer, or impersonate another account.

During sign-in, the App sends the backend service identity credentials, a device identifier (IDFV on iOS / ODID on OpenHarmony), and the app version. Signing out clears the local account sign-in session and locks AI.

On iOS / OpenHarmony, you can delete the backend service account from the account page. Deletion clears the account session and pending tickets, and unused credits cannot be restored; it does not delete the local ledger, iCloud data, or Android custom model configuration. iCloud data and remote platform account data must be managed separately under Apple, Huawei, or the backend service's rules.

后端服务额度仅适用于 iOS 和 OpenHarmony。额度以 Credits 显示，1 Credit 等于 10000 后端额度单位；每次 AI 请求按实际模型和用量扣减，不同模型消耗不同。剩余额度、订单和额度流水可在账户页查看。

Credits 不是法定货币，不可提现、转让或跨账号转移。

5. AI-assisted bookkeeping credits and billing

Backend service credits exist only on iOS and OpenHarmony. Credits are displayed in Credits, where 1 Credit equals 10,000 backend quota units. Each AI request deducts based on the actual model and usage, and different models consume different amounts. Remaining credits, orders, and credit activity appear on the account page.

Credits are not legal currency and cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or moved across accounts.
