Scope and statement

This policy applies to the DugaLedger app and this privacy policy page. DugaLedger is provided by the DugaLedger Team (“we”), which is the controller of the relevant personal information. The app provides local bookkeeping, statistics, personalized display, optional AI-assisted bookkeeping, optional iCloud Sync, voice input, image recognition, and share import.

Android requires no account. On iOS / OpenHarmony, using AI requires signing in with Apple / Huawei and connecting to the backend service. Please read this policy before use. Before you first enter the main features, the app will ask you to accept this policy.

摘要

本应用以本地记账为核心，账本、预算、主题、设置和 AI 会话默认保存在设备本地。只有在您使用平台登录、AI、iCloud 同步、内购、系统语音/识别或主动导出/分享时，相关信息才会按本政策在设备、Apple、华为、后端服务、模型提供商或您选择的位置之间处理。

Android 端无平台账号和后端服务额度；iOS / OpenHarmony 端登录后由后端服务提供账户、钱包、订单、额度流水和 AI 网关配置。应用未集成广告、分析或追踪 SDK，不出售个人信息，也不将数据用于跨应用追踪。

Summary

DugaLedger is built around local bookkeeping. Ledger data, budgets, themes, settings, and AI sessions are stored locally by default. Information is processed between your device, Apple, Huawei, the backend service, model providers, or a location you choose only when you use platform sign-in, AI, iCloud Sync, purchases, system speech/recognition, or an export/share action.

Android has no platform account and no backend service credits. On iOS / OpenHarmony, the backend service provides the account, wallet, orders, credit activity, and AI gateway configuration after sign-in. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not use data for cross-app tracking.

1. Data inventory and purposes

We process information only as needed for the following features:

  • Local bookkeeping and personalization: entries, categories, budgets, balance snapshots, themes, language, and display settings for bookkeeping, statistics, and display.
  • Platform account iOS OpenHarmony: Apple / Huawei identity credentials, nonce or authorization code, device identifier (iOS IDFV / OpenHarmony ODID), app version, and the account profile, wallet, orders, credit activity, and AI gateway configuration returned by the backend service, for sign-in, account display, credits, and AI availability.

  • Purchases and credits iOS OpenHarmony: the store processes payment; the app and the backend service process product identifiers, transaction identifiers, receipt verification, orders, credits, and usage records.

The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or the installed-app list, and does not use such data for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking.

2. Local data and cloud boundaries

The app stores data in its local database, app preferences/key-value storage, and app support directory:

  • Entries, categories, budgets, balance snapshots, and their sync metadata
  • Language, display, theme, auto-category, and AI display settings
  • AI rules, custom themes, and Android model-provider name, Base URL, model, extra headers, and API Key
  • iOS / OpenHarmony account display data, backend service session tokens, and pending purchase tickets
  • AI session snapshots, session index, UI-only session records, and chat images
  • iOS App Group shared and shortcut images, Android filesDir/floating_ball_images floating-ball screenshots, OpenHarmony cacheDir/shared_image_inbox shared copies

On Android, API Keys are stored as plaintext in the local key-value store luanhua_kv.db, and model configuration is included in a user-initiated backup. On iOS / OpenHarmony, backend service tokens are stored as plaintext in app preferences (SharedPreferences), not in Keychain/Keystore; account profile data and pending tickets are also local, while the backend service's AI gateway key exists only in memory.

Deleting a conversation deletes its AI snapshot, UI records, and chat images. Ledger content is stored locally and is sent to the relevant services described below only when you actually use AI, iCloud, export/share, or platform-account features.

3. User-initiated backups

You may export ledger data, categories, budgets, balance snapshots, themes, AI rules, auto-category settings, and AI display settings as a plaintext JSON file, and Android exports may also include custom model-provider configuration. The file also carries metadata such as the format version, export platform, app version, and export time. The app shows a clear warning before export. You are responsible for securely storing, transferring, and deleting the file. Keep it in a trusted location and delete it when no longer needed.

4. 平台账号与后端服务

Android 无需账号，也不提供后端服务额度或 AI 网关账号。iOS 通过 Apple 登录，OpenHarmony 通过华为账号登录，随后绑定后端服务。

登录时，应用向后端服务发送 Apple 身份令牌与 nonce 或华为授权码、设备标识（iOS 为 IDFV，OpenHarmony 为 ODID）和应用版本；后端服务返回账户资料、钱包、订单、额度流水及 AI 网关配置。账户标识、昵称和头像地址用于显示当前账号、判断 AI 是否可用。

4. Platform Accounts and the Backend Service

Android requires no account and offers no backend service credits or AI gateway account. iOS uses Sign in with Apple, while OpenHarmony uses a Huawei account, then binds the backend service.

During sign-in, the app sends the backend service the Apple identity token and nonce or Huawei authorization code, device identifier (IDFV on iOS, ODID on OpenHarmony), and app version. The backend service returns the account profile, wallet, orders, credit activity, and AI gateway configuration. The account identifier, nickname, and avatar URL are used to display the current account and determine AI availability.

Backend service tokens are kept in local app preferences. Signing out clears the local account sign-in session. Deleting the account clears the account session and pending tickets, but does not delete the local ledger, iCloud data, or Android custom model configuration.

5. AI 辅助记账

Android AI 使用您配置的 OpenAI-compatible 提供商；模型配置和 API Key 以明文保存在本地键值存储，并在您主动导出模型配置时进入备份。iOS / OpenHarmony 登录后使用后端服务下发的模型、网关地址、密钥和额外请求头；网关密钥仅驻留内存，不持久化、不写日志。

发起 AI 回合时，应用可能向实际处理请求的后端服务网关或模型提供商发送：当前消息和此前会话上下文、应用系统提示、设备时间与时区、启用的自定义规则、分类名称摘要、工具定义、模型生成的工具调用及其结果、工具所需账本/分类数据和需要模型读取的图片。

Android 无本地文字识别时，图片会以图片数据传输给模型；iOS / OpenHarmony 仅在模型需要读取图片时发送图片数据。远端服务按其隐私政策处理这些数据；请避免输入不必要的敏感信息。

AI 工具可以直接创建、修改或删除账本记录，不会逐条等待您确认；模型输出也可能错误。请在使用后核对账本、分类、预算和余额结果。

5. AI-assisted bookkeeping

On Android, AI uses the OpenAI-compatible provider you configure; model configuration and API Keys are kept as plaintext in local key-value storage and enter a backup when you export model configuration. On iOS / OpenHarmony, the app uses the model, gateway address, key, and extra headers issued by the backend service after sign-in; the gateway key exists only in memory and is never persisted or logged.

When an AI turn starts, the app may send the following to the backend service's gateway or the model provider that actually handles the request: the current message and prior conversation context, app system prompt, device time and time zone, enabled custom rules, category-name digest, tool definitions, model-generated tool calls and results, ledger/category data required by tools, and images the model must read.

On Android, when no local text recognition is available, the image is sent to the model as image data. On iOS / OpenHarmony, image data is sent only when the model needs to read it. Remote services process this data under their own privacy policies; avoid unnecessary sensitive information.

AI tools can directly create, update, or delete ledger records without a per-record confirmation step, and model output may be wrong. Check ledger entries, categories, budgets, and balances after use.

6. 图片、语音与临时文件

聊天图片保存在应用支持目录的会话子目录（ai_chat_images）。会话快照移除图片二进制但保留本地图片引用；删除会话时，图片、快照及该会话记录一并删除。

  • iOS 分享扩展、相册、相机或“嘟嘎截屏记账”快捷指令产生的临时副本可能进入 App Group 的 ShareOcrImages 或临时目录；分享队列最多保留 5 个待处理会话，未被引用的文件约 10 分钟后清理，相机/选择图片的临时文件在读取后删除。
  • Android 悬浮球截屏存于 filesDir/floating_ball_images，最多 5 个待处理会话，未被引用的文件约 10 分钟后清理；相机临时文件位于 cacheDir/ai_chat_capture，读取后删除。
  • OpenHarmony 分享副本存于 cacheDir/shared_image_inbox，应用侧待处理会话队列最多保留 5 个会话；未被采用的副本约 10 分钟后清理，规范化副本在导入后删除。

首次同意本政策前，应用不读取这些外部分享或悬浮球队列。图片文字识别由系统能力或模型完成；任何需要模型读取的图片都会发送给实际 AI 服务。

语音输入调用系统语音服务，可能联网处理；应用不保存录音，只保留最终转写文本。拍照、选图、导入和导出使用系统相机、相册选择器、文件选择器或分享扩展，不申请广泛存储权限。

6. Images, voice, and temporary files

Chat images are stored in per-conversation folders under the app support directory (ai_chat_images). Session snapshots remove image bytes but retain a local image reference; deleting a conversation deletes its images, snapshot, and records together.

  • iOS Temporary copies from the share extension, photo library, camera, or the “Duga Screenshot Ledger” shortcut may enter the App Group ShareOcrImages directory or temporary directory. The share queue keeps at most 5 pending sessions; unreferenced files are cleared after about 10 minutes, and camera/selected-image temporary files are deleted after reading.
  • Android Floating-ball screenshots go to filesDir/floating_ball_images, with at most 5 pending sessions and unreferenced files cleared after about 10 minutes; camera temporary files go to cacheDir/ai_chat_capture and are deleted after reading.
  • OpenHarmony Shared copies go to cacheDir/shared_image_inbox; the app-side pending-session queue keeps at most 5 sessions, unreferenced copies are cleared after about 10 minutes, and normalized copies are deleted after import.

Before you first accept this policy, the app does not read these external-share or floating-ball queues. Image text recognition is performed by system capability or the model; any picture the model must read is sent to the actual AI service.

Voice input uses the system speech service, which may process audio online; the app does not retain recordings and keeps only the final transcript. Camera, photo, import, and export actions use the system camera, photo picker, file picker, or share extension, and the app does not request broad storage access.

7. iCloud 同步 iOS

您可主动开启 iCloud 同步。同步内容为收支记录、分类、预算、余额快照、同步状态及同步元数据，保存于 Apple CloudKit 容器 iCloud.com.soloyehaha.luanhua，并在同一 Apple ID 的设备间同步。同步默认关闭；关闭后本地数据继续保留。

7. iCloud Sync iOS

You may enable iCloud Sync. It synchronizes entries, categories, budgets, balance snapshots, sync state, and sync metadata through the Apple CloudKit container iCloud.com.soloyehaha.luanhua across devices signed into the same Apple ID. Sync is off by default; turning it off keeps local data.

AI sessions, chat images, model configuration, account tokens, and purchase tickets are not stored in iCloud. Apple's rules govern iCloud processing, transport encryption, and retention.
